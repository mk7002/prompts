# Landscape Generation

## Description

Generate detailed, realistic landscape and scenery images with natural lighting and depth.

## Prompt

> Generate a breathtaking landscape of [scene description: location type, key features such as mountains, lake, forest, coastline]. Capture it at [time of day, e.g. sunrise / golden hour / blue hour / night] with [weather or atmosphere, e.g. clear sky / mist / dramatic clouds].
>
> Create a strong sense of depth with a clear foreground, midground, and background. Use natural, physically plausible lighting, accurate reflections, and realistic textures for terrain, water, and vegetation.
>
> Compose the image using balanced framing and leading lines. Keep colors rich but natural, and avoid oversaturation, repeated patterns, or unrealistic elements.

## Use Cases

- Wallpapers and backgrounds
- Travel and tourism visuals
- Blog and presentation imagery
- Scene and environment concepts

## Notes

Specify aspect ratio (e.g. 16:9 for wallpapers, 9:16 for phone backgrounds). Naming a lens or shot type (wide-angle, aerial, panoramic) helps control perspective.
