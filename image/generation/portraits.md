# Portrait Generation

## Description

Generate a realistic, well-lit portrait of a person with natural skin, accurate anatomy, and a clean composition.

## Prompt

> Generate a high-quality portrait of [subject description: age, appearance, clothing, expression]. Frame the shot as a [headshot / half-body / full-body] portrait with the subject in sharp focus and a softly blurred background of [setting].
>
> Use [lighting style, e.g. soft natural window light / studio three-point lighting / golden-hour sunlight] that flatters the face and creates natural shadows. Render realistic skin texture, natural eyes with catchlights, accurate hands and proportions, and believable hair detail.
>
> Keep the composition clean and balanced, with a natural pose and genuine expression. Avoid distorted features, extra fingers, plastic-looking skin, over-smoothing, or artificial artifacts.

## Use Cases

- Profile pictures and avatars
- Character concepts and personas
- Marketing and website imagery
- Headshot-style reference images

## Notes

Replace the bracketed placeholders. Specific details (age range, clothing, lighting, lens such as 85mm) give more consistent results. Combine with a style from [image/styling](../styling/) for a specific look.
