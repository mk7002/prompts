# Product Photography

## Description

Generate clean, commercial-quality product images suitable for e-commerce, ads, and catalogs.

## Prompt

> Create a professional product photograph of [product description: type, color, material, brand-neutral details]. Place the product [on a plain white background / on a styled surface such as marble or wood / in a lifestyle setting of ...].
>
> Use soft, even studio lighting with controlled highlights and realistic shadows or reflections beneath the product. Keep the product in sharp focus, accurately shaped, and true to its described colors and materials.
>
> Compose the shot from a [front / three-quarter / top-down] angle with enough negative space for text if needed. Avoid distorted shapes, warped labels, invented logos, cluttered backgrounds, or unrealistic reflections.

## Use Cases

- E-commerce listings
- Advertising and social media creatives
- Catalog and packaging mockups
- Concept visuals for new products

## Notes

Image models often misrender text and logos, so add branding separately if exact text matters. For real products, use an image-editing prompt with a reference photo instead of generating from scratch.
