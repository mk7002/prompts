# Photorealistic Style

## Description

Make an image look like a real, unedited photograph taken with a professional camera.

## Prompt

> Render the image in a photorealistic style, indistinguishable from a real photograph taken with a professional camera and [lens, e.g. 35mm / 50mm / 85mm]. Use natural, physically accurate lighting, shadows, and reflections.
>
> Show realistic textures and imperfections such as skin pores, fabric weave, and surface wear. Use true-to-life colors, accurate proportions, and natural depth of field.
>
> Avoid an illustrated, painted, CGI, or overly polished look. No plastic skin, oversharpening, or artificial glow.

## Use Cases

- Making generated images look like real photos
- Product and lifestyle visuals
- Realistic reference images
- Correcting an overly artificial AI look

## Notes

Mentioning camera and lens details and specific lighting conditions improves realism. Combine with any [generation prompt](../generation/).
