# Professional Style

## Description

Give images a clean, polished, corporate-ready look for business and professional use.

## Prompt

> Style the image with a clean, professional, business-ready look. Use bright, even, flattering lighting, a neutral or softly blurred background, and a balanced composition.
>
> Keep colors natural and slightly muted with accurate skin tones. Make sure the subject looks well-groomed and confident, and keep clothing and identity unchanged.
>
> Keep the overall image polished but authentic, avoiding heavy retouching, dramatic effects, or distracting elements.

## Use Cases

- LinkedIn and resume headshots
- Corporate websites and team pages
- Business presentations and reports
- Professional marketing material

## Notes

When restyling a real photo, use it with an image-editing tool so the person's identity is preserved. Pair with [Background Replacement](../editing/background-replacement.md) for studio-style backdrops.
