# Artistic Style

## Description

Transform images into an artistic style such as painting, illustration, or sketch while keeping the subject recognizable.

## Prompt

> Transform the image into a [art style, e.g. oil painting / watercolor / pencil sketch / digital illustration / anime / impressionist] style. Apply the characteristic brushwork, textures, line quality, and color palette of that style consistently across the whole image.
>
> Keep the original composition, subject, and key features recognizable, including pose, proportions, and facial identity where applicable.
>
> Make the result look like a cohesive, intentional piece of art rather than a filter, avoiding muddy details, inconsistent style, or distorted features.

## Use Cases

- Artistic portraits and gifts
- Illustrations for blogs, books, or posters
- Stylized social media content
- Concept art and mood boards

## Notes

Naming a specific medium or art movement produces better results than general terms like "artistic". Avoid imitating specific living artists by name.
