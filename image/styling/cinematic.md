# Cinematic Style

## Description

Apply a cinematic, film-like look with dramatic lighting, color grading, and composition.

## Prompt

> Render the image in a cinematic style, like a still frame from a high-budget film. Use dramatic, motivated lighting with deep shadows and controlled highlights, and a [color grade, e.g. teal and orange / warm golden / cool desaturated / neon noir] palette.
>
> Apply a shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, and a widescreen composition with an anamorphic feel. Keep the subject and scene content unchanged; change only the lighting mood, color grading, and atmosphere.
>
> Keep the result natural and believable, avoiding over-processed HDR, heavy vignettes, or unrealistic colors.

## Use Cases

- Dramatic portraits and scenes
- Storytelling and poster visuals
- Music, film, and event promotions
- Mood boards

## Notes

Works as a modifier: append it to a generation prompt, or use it with an editing tool to restyle an existing photo. Name a film genre or era for a more specific look.
