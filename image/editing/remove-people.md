# Remove Unwanted People

## Description

Remove selected unwanted people from a photo while preserving the main subject and naturally reconstructing the background.

## Prompt

> Remove the selected unwanted people from the image. Reconstruct the background naturally so the removed areas seamlessly match the surrounding environment, including perspective, lighting, shadows, texture, color, and depth.
>
> Keep the main subject completely unchanged, including face, facial features, hair, body, clothes, pose, proportions, position, and expression. Preserve the original composition and image quality.

## Use Cases

- Removing tourists or bystanders from travel photos
- Removing photobombers
- Cleaning up event or group photos
- Isolating the main subject in crowded scenes

## Notes

Best used when the people to remove are clearly selected or masked. Large removals in complex scenes may need multiple passes. For general objects, see [Remove Unwanted Objects](./remove-objects.md).
