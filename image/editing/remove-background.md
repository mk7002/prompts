# Remove Background

## Description

Remove the background entirely and leave the subject on a transparent, white, or plain-colored background with clean edges.

## Prompt

> Remove the background completely and place the subject on a [transparent / pure white / plain color, e.g. light gray] background.
>
> Keep the subject exactly as it is, with no changes to shape, colors, details, or proportions. For people, keep the face, hair, skin tone, outfit, body, pose, and expression unchanged.
>
> Create clean, precise edges around the subject, including fine details such as hair, fur, and thin parts. Leave no halo, color fringing, shadows, or leftover background.

## Use Cases

- Product cut-outs for e-commerce listings
- Profile pictures and ID-style photos
- Stickers, logos, and design assets
- Isolating a subject to place in another design

## Notes

Transparent output depends on the tool: some AI image tools cannot export real transparency and add a white or checkered background instead. If you need a true transparent PNG, generate on pure white and then use a dedicated background remover. For a styled backdrop instead of a plain one, use [Studio Background](./studio-background.md) or [Background Replacement](./background-replacement.md).
