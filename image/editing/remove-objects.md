# Remove Unwanted Objects

## Description

Remove selected unwanted objects or distractions while preserving the main subject and original composition.

## Prompt

> Remove the selected unwanted objects or distractions from the image while preserving the main subject exactly as they are.
>
> Naturally reconstruct the removed areas so the background looks realistic and seamlessly blends with the surrounding scene. Match the existing background, perspective, lighting, shadows, colors, textures, and depth of field.
>
> Do not alter the main subject in any way. Keep their face, facial features, hair, body, clothes, pose, proportions, position, and expression unchanged.
>
> Do not modify any other part of the original image unnecessarily. Maintain the original composition, lighting, image quality, and overall appearance.

## Use Cases

- Removing unwanted objects such as bins, poles, cars, or signs
- Cleaning up travel and street photos
- Removing visual distractions from product shots
- Removing litter or clutter from a scene

## Notes

Best used when the unwanted areas are clearly selected or masked. For removing people, see [Remove Unwanted People](./remove-people.md).
