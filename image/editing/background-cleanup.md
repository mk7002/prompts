# Background Cleanup

## Description

Remove small distractions and clutter from the background while keeping the main subject and intended elements intact.

## Prompt

> Clean up the selected unwanted elements from the background while preserving the main subject and all intended elements of the image. Reconstruct the affected areas naturally and consistently with the surrounding scene. Match perspective, lighting, shadows, colors, textures, reflections, and depth of field. Do not change the main subject or alter unrelated parts of the image.

## Use Cases

- Removing clutter, signs, wires, or litter from backgrounds
- Cleaning up product or portrait backdrops
- Tidying real-estate and interior photos
- Removing stains, marks, or blemishes from walls and surfaces

## Notes

Best used when the unwanted elements are clearly selected or masked. For removing people specifically, see [Remove Unwanted People](./remove-people.md).
