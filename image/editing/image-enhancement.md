# Image Enhancement

## Description

Improve overall image quality—clarity, sharpness, exposure, and noise—without changing the content or identity.

## Prompt

> Enhance the image naturally while preserving the original identity, composition, details, colors, and overall appearance. Improve clarity, sharpness, exposure, and fine details, and reduce noise where appropriate without creating artificial textures, changing facial features, or introducing new objects.

## Use Cases

- Restoring low-light or noisy photos
- Sharpening slightly blurry images
- Improving old or low-resolution photos
- Preparing photos for print or publishing

## Notes

Enhancement cannot recover detail that is not present; heavy upscaling may invent texture. Mention specific issues (e.g. underexposed, noisy, soft focus) for more targeted results.
